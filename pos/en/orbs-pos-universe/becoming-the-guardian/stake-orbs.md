# Stake ORBS

## 1. Prepare Wallet

Login your guardian wallet. The wallet must be the same wallet you used to register a guardian.

## 2. Open TETRA - Orbs staking wallet

* PC: Open [TETRA](https://staking.orbs.network/) with Chrome browser.
* Mobile: Install TETRA MOBILE application. ([Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.orbs.pos.klip) / [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/tetra-mobile/id6453168712?l=en-GB))

Connect your wallet (ie. Metamask) to TETRA

## 3. Stake ORBS

1. Transfer ORBS tokens from exchange or another source.
2. Transfer ETH for gas fee to approve required transaction during staking.
3. Connect Metamask to Tetra.&#x20;
4. Click "STAKE" button and follow the instruction.
   1. Select a guardian you delegate
   2. Approve token transfer to staking contract
   3. Execute staking contract function

{% hint style="info" %}
Unstake ORBS requires 14 days cooldown.
{% endhint %}
